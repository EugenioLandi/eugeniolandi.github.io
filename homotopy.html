Model Categories and Abstract Homotopy Theory

Course Syllabus: Model Categories

This course develops the language of model categories, homotopy categories, standard examples, diagram model structures, and simplicial localization.

Lecture 1 — Relative Categories and Model Categories

  • Relative categories and localization: wide subcategories, relative functors, weak equivalences, 2-out-of-3, and localization by zig-zags.
  • Model category axioms: arrow categories, retracts, functorial factorizations, lifting properties, and the definition of a model structure.
  • Cofibrant and fibrant objects: initial/terminal objects, replacements, and the cofibrant/fibrant subcategories.
  • Basic results: retract argument, lifting characterizations, closure under pushout/pullback, and Ken Brown's lemma.

Lecture 2 — Homotopy and the Homotopy Category

  • Localization of bifibrant objects: equivalences between C, Cc, Cf, and Ccf after inverting weak equivalences.
  • Cylinder and path objects: left/right homotopy, homotopy equivalence, and functorial cylinder/path constructions.
  • Homotopy theory of maps: functoriality, equivalence relations, and invariance under weak equivalence.
  • Homotopy category: quotient by homotopy, identification of weak and homotopy equivalences on bifibrant objects, and computation of morphisms in Ho(C).

Lecture 3 — Standard Examples and Quillen Theory

  • Top and simplicial sets: weak homotopy equivalences, Serre fibrations, generating cofibrations, horns, Kan fibrations, realization, and singular complexes.
  • Quillen adjunctions: left/right Quillen functors and Quillen adjunctions.
  • Derived functors: total left/right derived functors and derived adjunctions.
  • Quillen equivalences: characterizations and the Quillen equivalence between Top and sSet via realization and singular complex.

Lecture 4 — Diagram Categories and Homotopy (Co)Limits

  • Limits and colimits: why ordinary limits/colimits fail to preserve weak equivalences and how to derive them.
  • Projective and injective structures: objectwise weak equivalences, projective fibrations, and injective cofibrations.
  • Reedy model structures: Reedy categories, latching/matching objects, and Reedy cofibrations/fibrations.
  • Properness: left and right proper model categories and slice-category criteria.
  • Homotopy pushouts/pullbacks: derived pushouts and pullbacks via cofibrant/fibrant replacement.

Lecture 5 — From Model Categories to (∞,1)-Categories

  • ∞-groupoids: higher morphisms, invertibility, and the fundamental ∞-groupoid of a space.
  • Enriched and higher categories: monoidal enrichment, groupoids, (2,1)-categories, and (∞,1)-categories.
  • From categories to simplicial categories: comonads from adjunctions and the functor J: Cat → SCat.
  • Simplicial localization: localization of a relative category, Reedy structures on simplicial objects, simplicial frames, and mapping spaces.
